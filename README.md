## 🧭 What Is This?

dsh-hold-to-talk turns your mouse into a microphone. It's a tiny helper for the DeepSeek Harness web composer. Instead of typing long messages, you simply hold your mouse button down on the input box, speak naturally, release the mouse, and your words appear as text. No coding, no complicated setup, no stickers or buttons needed.

**Here's the magic:**
- 🖱️ **Hold** your left mouse button on the text input area.
- 🗣️ **Speak** whatever you want to say(at your normal pace).
- ✍️ **Release** the mouse button—your words instantly appear in the text box.

## 🎤 How to Use It

Using it is as easy as 1-2-3:

1. **Open the DeepSeek Harness web composer** in your browser. (This is the webpage where you normally type your messages or prompts.)
2. **Place your mouse cursor** over the text input box where you'd normally type.
.
3. **Press and hold** your left mouse button. Start speaking immediately. You'll see a small floating window appear showing your words in real time—like magic.
4. **When you're done speaking**, simply **release** the mouse button. Your words will be inserted into the draft exactly where the cursor was.
.

**Special Trick:**
- If you want to **cancel** what you just spoke, don't release over the input box. Instead, keeping the mouse button held down, **drag upward** (move the mouse up, off the input box),then release. The text will be discarded,and nothing will be added to your draft. This is your "oops, scratch that" move.

.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting (Just in Case)

Here are quick fixes for the most common hiccups:

### ❌ The floating window doesn't appear when I hold the mouse?
- Make sure the app is actually running. Check your system tray (bottom-right corner).If you see the icon,you're good. If not, go back to the extracted folder and double-click the app again.
.- **Restart your browser.** Sometimes a fresh browser session helps the app connect properly.

### 🎤 It's listening but my words don't show up?
- Speak **clearly at a normal volume**. No need to shout—just your regular speaking voice is perfect.
.- Make sure your microphone is **not muted** and is **selected as the default device** in Windows Sound settings.
.- If you're in a noisy environment, try moving closer to your microphoneor using a headset mic.

.

### 🐢 The text appears slowly?
- First-time setup may take a few seconds to load the speech model. After that,it becomes faster. Give it about 10-20 seconds after the first launch before you try speaking. You'll see a "ready" notification from the tray icon when it's fully loaded.
